\chapter{Derivative-Based Parser}
\label{sec:derivative-parser}

Chapter~\ref{sec:matching-vs-parsing} fixed what is being computed --
the $\geq_r$-maximal parse tree of Definition~\ref{sec:posix-longest-leftmost}
-- without saying how. This chapter presents Sulzmann and Lu's answer
\citep{sulzmannlu2014}: a parser built directly on the Brzozowski
derivative of Section~\ref{sec:brzozowski-derivatives}, in two forms. The
first, direct construction computes a derivative sequence and then
explicitly rebuilds a parse tree from it; the second, bit-coded
construction fuses parse-tree bookkeeping into the derivative step itself.
Both are developed here as theory first, then as the two Rust
implementations that realize them, \texttt{src/posix/standard/} and
\texttt{src/posix/bitcoded/}. Chapter~\ref{sec:partial-derivative-parser}
repeats the exercise once more, replacing the Brzozowski derivative
throughout with Antimirov's partial derivative from
Section~\ref{sec:antimirov-partial-derivatives}.
