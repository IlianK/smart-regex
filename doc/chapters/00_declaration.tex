\chapter*{Declaration of Authorship}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Declaration of Authorship}

I hereby declare that I have written the present Master's thesis
independently and without unauthorized assistance from third parties. 
I have used only the sources and resources listed in the thesis. 
All passages taken directly or indirectly from
published or unpublished works have been clearly identified as such. 
This thesis has not previously been submitted, 
in whole or in part, in the same or a similar form, 
to any other examination authority, nor has it been published.
